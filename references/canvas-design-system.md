# Canvas design system notes

This is a lightweight visual-design philosophy for polished storefront work.

## Principles

- favor clear hierarchy over decoration
- let spacing and contrast guide attention
- keep type, image treatment, and color aligned with the brand
- do not overuse effects or motion on commerce pages
- aim for a clean, premium feel without becoming generic

## Good storefront composition

- generous whitespace around the hero and product sections
- a restrained accent color used intentionally
- media aligned to the message rather than filling the page for decoration alone
- consistent card and section shapes

This helps the site feel designed rather than assembled from random template blocks.
